Privacy Policy

Application Name: Mihrap

Last Updated: 2026

Mihrap (“the Application”) values user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the application.

1. Information Collected

Mihrap is an application that does not require user registration. No personal information such as name, surname, email address, or account details is collected.

a) Location Information

Approximate location information may be used to ensure the proper functioning of the following features:

  • Prayer times
  • Qibla finder
  • Ramadan prayer timetable (Imsakiyah)

Location information is used only on the device, is not stored on servers, and is not shared with third parties.

b) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The application uses Google AdMob advertising services. AdMob may collect the following data:

  • Device information
  • Ad interaction data
  • Approximate location information
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)

This data does not contain personally identifiable information and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

c) Premium Membership (In-App Purchases)

Premium membership transactions are processed through the Apple App Store. Payment information is not accessed or stored by the application.

2. Purposes of Data Use

  • Providing application features
  • Displaying advertisements
  • Offering premium content
  • Improving application performance

3. Data Sharing

Mihrap does not sell user data or share it with third parties without consent. Data may be shared in a limited manner only with the following service providers:

  • Google AdMob
  • Apple (for in-app purchase transactions)

4. Children’s Privacy

Mihrap does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If such a situation is identified, please contact us.

5. Data Security

Appropriate technical and administrative measures are taken to protect user privacy. However, it cannot be guaranteed that data transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page.
